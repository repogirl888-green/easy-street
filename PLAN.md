## Easy Street Plus

- **Challenge levels:** Choose your starting level: Starter (1,000 points), Pro (100,000 points), or Big Leagues (1,000,000 points). Stakes scale with your balance, so bigger levels feel bigger.
- **Weekly Market Read:** A plain-English recap of what moved the market and why.
- **Street School:** Short guided lessons for beginners.
- **Moving Day:** A daily feed of the market's biggest movers, explained in plain English. *Movin' On Up* shows what went up, with a little moving truck driving toward the house on Easy Street. *Movin' Out* shows what went down, with the truck driving away. Ups and downs always get equal space.

**Pricing (planned):** $2.99 to $3.99 a month, or a discounted yearly plan, with a free 7-day trial. The core app stays free.

**Staying above board:** Easy Street Plus is a learning upgrade, not a way to buy points. Points are never sold, can't be cashed out, and have no value outside the app. Challenge levels change the practice, not the prize. Moving Day shares information about what's moving and why. It never tells anyone what to buy or sell.


## Launch order

2. Build interest and users.
3. Add real market data for Moving Day and real results for the game.
4. Set up Google Play subscriptions.
